- Why it's worth doing / angle: Ordinary readers strongly associate AI with "a black box"; this is a rare scientific good-news story that can be explained clearly. Use the cat-and-car example to explain "why we used to misread it", then why being able to read a model's internals helps safety. Separate the original conclusions from the Chinese community's extended interpretations.
- Timeliness and verification: the Chinese post is within 24 hours; the English original is 24–48 hours. Checked against [the original essay "Neural Networks Are Already Clean"](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/): the author labels it a "working hypothesis"; confirmed that in preliminary audits of Qwen3-32B roughly 90% of neuron poles have one main job and about 7% have two related jobs; about 99% of the ConvNeXt last layer is interpretable (the original 2014 InceptionV1 is about 50% with new tools, about 70% retrained); the cat-car neuron 4e:55 corresponds to a V shape; removing the weakest 75% of activations drops accuracy from 72% to 71%, while removing the strongest 7% drops it to 4%. MaxForAI's "99%–100% in the last layer" refers to modern vision models, not all models.
