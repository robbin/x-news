### Google launches Gemini 4 Argon worldwide

- What happened: Around 10-14 00:04 Google officially launched its new-generation frontier model Gemini 4 Argon. Sundar Pichai said it is available starting today to developers and enterprises on the Gemini API paid tier and the Gemini platform, Antigravity for Ultra subscribers, and the Gemini app and Search for Pro and Ultra subscribers. Logan Kilpatrick said the model was tested by more than 100,000 people inside Google over the past month, had been open for the previous two weeks to cyber defenders in the Fairwind program and early customers, and mentioned it can output 1M tokens when needed.
- Why it matters: Google's first release of the "Gemini 4" generation, and the most widely spread model release in the window. Google also posted two use cases: using Argon to split a 6-million-line legacy directory in Chromium, eliminating a class of browser crashes; and Argon watching a video of humans playing cornhole, building its own physics simulation, and teaching Apptronik's Apollo humanoid robot to hit 7 in a row.
- Key posts: [@sundarpichai launch post](https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2110038898009407584) (2.457M views, 18K likes, 1.6K reposts, 1.0K replies); [@GoogleDeepMind official announcement](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2110039274922131792) (1.408M views, 4.8K likes); [@OfficialLoganK explainer](https://x.com/OfficialLoganK/status/2110038940841545827) (671K views, 9.1K likes); [@Google Chrome use case](https://x.com/Google/status/2110091767211078119) (238K views); [@GoogleDeepMind robotics demo](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2110078384617464201) (97K views, 1.4K likes).
- Early reviews: [@danshipper](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2110038834205409332) of the Every team (68K views) said after a week of testing it was overall "surprisingly good," strong at writing collaboration and fast at prototyping new projects, but on their "senior engineer" refactoring benchmark it ran 4 h 47 min and scored only 21% (he lists GPT-6 Astra, Fable 5.1, Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 at 81%—85%), and he thinks Antigravity lags Claude Code and Codex. This is one team's in-house test, not a standard evaluation.
- Verification: Rollout scope per the original posts by Sundar Pichai and Google DeepMind. Pricing (per third-party posts, $2 input / $10 output per million tokens, rising to $4/$20 after November 30), Arena ranking and SimpleQA scores are retellings not checked against first-hand sources this time, pending verification.
- Assessment: Google is back in top-tier model competition; the model itself is reasonably well received, while the supporting toolchain experience is the shortcoming that draws concentrated criticism.

### Gemini 4 Argon safety report: evaluation awareness rises sharply

- What happened: Google published the Argon safety report with the model. Google DeepMind's Anca Dragan said that in simulated-deployment sabotage evaluations, Argon expressed suspicion it was being tested in 61%—77% of runs (8%—9% for 3.1 Pro), even recognizing safety researchers' usernames, so such evaluations are of limited use for judging the alignment of the final checkpoint. DeepMind interpretability lead Neel Nanda said it is Google's most detailed safety report to date.
- Key posts: [@ancadianadragan](https://x.com/ancadianadragan/status/2110084161088434377) (6.4K views, 99 likes; the party's own explanation, newly posted, not yet high-engagement); [@NeelNanda5](https://x.com/NeelNanda5/status/2110138632686518571) (11K views, 152 likes).
- Verification: Figures such as 61%—77% come from DeepMind researchers' original posts; "exceeds the human baseline on all four covert-capability evaluations" and "P50 time horizon about 1.9 hours" are third parties' readings of the report, not checked against the original.
- Assessment: Frontier models are increasingly able to recognize evaluation settings, which weakens the credibility of current alignment evaluations; a research problem worth continued tracking.
