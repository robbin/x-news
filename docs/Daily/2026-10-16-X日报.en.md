# X.com AI News Daily (2026-10-16)

## 1. AI models, products and applications

### Meta releases Muse Spark Open weights, partners with Apple to run on Mac Studio

- What happened: Meta released Muse Spark Open, the open-weight version of its flagship Muse Spark, calling it "the most capable American open-weight model yet." Specs: sparse MoE, 816B total parameters, 42B active per token (8 of 256 experts), 1M-token context, native text, image, video and audio input (text output), aimed at coding and long-horizon agent tasks. Weights are on Hugging Face in BF16 and NVFP4 formats under Meta's own Muse Model License. Zuckerberg said it was done with Apple and can run on the new Mac Studio; Meta's developer docs state it can run on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios via MLX tensor parallelism.
- Why it matters: The highest-engagement AI event in the window. Meta had previously kept the Muse series mainly on its own API (Muse Wonder launched around 10-13, and Agent Arena gave it 12th place on 10-15); this time it released the weights of the same-generation flagship and publicly partnered with Apple on local inference.
- Key posts: [@finkd Zuckerberg](https://x.com/finkd/status/2110778025629347999) (10-16 01:01, 1.223M views, 19K likes, 1.0K reposts, 723 replies); [@AIatMeta official release](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2110779754274292212) (10-16 01:08, 571K views, 8.6K likes, 895 reposts); NVIDIA's supplementary training info at [@NVIDIAAI](https://x.com/NVIDIAAI/status/2110799083749495190) (says trained on GB300 NVL72, 12K views).
- Verification: Parameters, context, modalities, formats and license checked against the [Hugging Face model card](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Spark-Open-816B-A42B), the [Meta research blog](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open) and [Meta developer docs](https://dev.meta.ai/docs/muse-spark-open). The model card states self-hosting needs at least about 700GB of GPU memory (NVFP4) or 2TB (BF16); NVFP4 weights are about 537GB and can be served on a single 4-GPU Blackwell node. "Most capable American open weights" is Meta's own claim. A user noted the license requires a "Built with Muse Spark Open" label in the interface and has separate commercial terms for AI service providers above a certain scale ([@Ldataengineer](https://x.com/Ldataengineer/status/2110794302674891055), low engagement); specific terms per the official license. Speed figures such as about 25 tok/s on two Mac Studios are low-engagement retellings, not verified.
- Assessment: This is open weights, not open source in the licensing sense; its real significance is lowering the bar for a frontier-class MoE model to "runs locally on two high-end Macs," but for the vast majority of individual developers it remains an API-first model.

### Microsoft AI: 2.8T-parameter base MAI-Base-2 finishes pre-training

- What happened: Microsoft AI (MAI) head Mustafa Suleyman announced the team has a fully in-house frontier pre-trained base at the 3T-parameter scale, with post-training results "coming soon." The technical blog discloses MAI-Base-2 is an MoE with 2.8T total and 80B active parameters, trained for 38 days on 16,000 GB200s in August—September 2026 on 50T tokens, natively visual (encoder-free), with a March 2026 knowledge cutoff.
- Why it matters: Microsoft's first public showing of a base model built in-house to frontier scale, outside its OpenAI partnership; the blog stresses no distillation, all data processed in-house, and plans to license weights to trusted partners via Foundry.
- Key post: [@mustafasuleyman](https://x.com/mustafasuleyman/status/2110766651344146564) (10-16 00:16, 509K views, 2.2K likes, 203 reposts).
- Verification: Checked against the [Microsoft AI technical blog](https://microsoft.ai/news/frontier-pre-training-at-microsoft-ai/), confirming 2.8T/80B, 16k GB200, 38 days, 50T tokens, average MFU 21%, goodput 89%, and that "4.2—12.3x more pre-training FLOPs-efficient than DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base" are all Microsoft self-tests (compared using bits-per-byte on in-house/commissioned eval sets, and some comparison models only have post-trained versions).
- Assessment: For now it is only a base, with no usable product or public benchmarks; "4—12x more efficient" is an estimate extrapolated from Microsoft's own scaling laws and should not be compared directly with finished models until the post-trained model is out.

### NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and the local Bolt model

- What happened: NVIDIA released the open multimodal model Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (120B total parameters, 12B active, Mamba-2/Transformer hybrid MoE, up to 1M context, image, document and video understanding), along with the local-oriented Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB (native 2-bit quantization, fits in 24GB of memory); the smaller Bolt E4B 4GB is scheduled for October 23.
- Why it matters: Artificial Analysis said on launch night that Super VL, at 26 points, became "the leading American open-weight model"; but about an hour later Meta released Muse Spark Open and the title was immediately challenged. Some in the Chinese circle joked about this.
- Key posts: [@NVIDIAAI official](https://x.com/NVIDIAAI/status/2110749836450713896) (10-15 23:09, 346K views, 3.6K likes, 432 reposts); third-party evaluation [@ArtificialAnlys](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2110760441240752399) (90K views, 795 likes); Bolt 24GB at [@NVIDIAAI](https://x.com/NVIDIAAI/status/2110824076218605922) (27K views, 872 likes).
- Verification: Parameters, architecture, context and the Bolt E4B date checked against the [NVIDIA official blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-3-5-super-vl/) and the [Hugging Face model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL-120B-A12B-BF16) (license OpenMDW-1.1). A user pointed out that in NVIDIA's own comparison table Qwen3.8 Flash-Next wins all six shared benchmarks ([@superalesha](https://x.com/superalesha/status/2110745129883767052), 15K views); this is a user's reading of the official table, not independently checked.
- Assessment: Super VL's selling points are per-token efficiency and enterprise customizability, while Bolt targets local devices such as RTX Spark; the "strongest American open model" label itself means little.

- Key post: [@AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2110784662071398807) (10-16 01:27, 250K views, 2.2K likes, 160 reposts).

- Assessment: A rare concrete arrangement by a frontier lab to "embed" external auditing; next, watch how much real information METR's first public report contains.
